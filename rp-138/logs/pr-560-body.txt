## What

The MCP page's Clients tab (behind `cloud-project-entities`, off by default) shows a world map of caller countries above the existing country table.

- `components/clients/CountryMap.tsx`: a d3-geo Equal Earth map from world-atlas `countries-110m`. It loads through `next/dynamic` from the Clients tab only, so the geometry ships in its own chunk.
- The countries are colored by calls on one sequential blue (`chart-1`, DESIGN.md) in four log-scale buckets, with a legend. Countries with no calls stay neutral. The Unknown row and the DB-IP attribution stay below the map.
- Hover shows the ds tooltip with the country name, calls and sessions. Each colored country is a focusable link with an `aria-label`. A click opens the Traces sessions list with `facetCountry`, and keeps the window, the environment and the server filter.
- `lib/clients/country-codes.ts`: ISO alpha-2 to numeric (all 249 codes; withdrawn codes such as `UK` read as their successor; Kosovo matched by name).
- `lib/clients/country-map-model.ts`: the color buckets and the row join.
- `mcpCountryTracesHref` in `lib/mcp-servers/paths.ts`, and the client event `client_country_clicked`.
- No backend change. With the flag off, the Clients tab stays not found, and Traces and the MCP page do not change.
